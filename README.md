# Automobile Supply Chain Analytics

## Project Overview
This project presents a data-driven comparative analysis of supply chain performance, variability and risk in the Indian automobile sector, focusing on three major manufacturers: Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai Motor India and Toyota Kirloskar Motor.

The study examines how differences in supply chain strategies influence supplier reliability, operational efficiency, lead-time variability and disruption risks.

## Project Objectives
- Compare supplier performance across the three automobile manufacturers.
- Analyse supply chain variability and operational stability.
- Examine demand, production and inventory behaviour.
- Evaluate supplier risk using clustering techniques.
- Identify supply chain disruptions through quantitative and qualitative analysis.

## Companies Covered
- Maruti Suzuki India Ltd.
- Hyundai Motor India
- Toyota Kirloskar Motor

## Tools & Technologies
- Python / R (include only if used in the submitted analysis)
- Power BI
- ARIMA Time Series Forecasting
- K-Means Clustering
- NLP-Based Risk Analysis
- Statistical Analysis and Data Visualisation

## Methodology
The study combines multiple analytical approaches:

1. Statistical diagnostics to examine supply chain performance and variability.
2. Time series forecasting using ARIMA.
3. K-Means clustering for supplier segmentation.
4. NLP-based analysis of qualitative supply chain risks.
5. Power BI visualisations for comparative analysis.

## Key Areas of Analysis
- Supplier reliability
- Lead-time variability
- Production stability
- Inventory efficiency
- Forecast accuracy
- Supply chain disruption patterns

## Project Deliverable
The repository contains the academic research report documenting the comparative analysis, methodology and findings.

## Conclusion
The study highlights the differences in supply chain approaches across the three automobile manufacturers and combines quantitative performance indicators with qualitative risk analysis to assess supply chain resilience and operational maturity.
